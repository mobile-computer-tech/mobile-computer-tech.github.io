About Me

I am a recently retired Silicon Valley engineer with a lifelong passion for computer hardware and software, dating back to 1984. Throughout my career, I have developed extensive software solutions and led teams of engineers and IT professionals in cutting-edge technology startup companies. I am confident in my ability to efficiently diagnose and resolve complex technical issues that may require significant resources to address.

After several years of enjoying early retirement, I realized the value of applying my skills to benefit my local community and foster meaningful engagement. I launched this venture with the primary goal of giving back and connecting with others.

While I would ideally offer my services pro bono, my wife encourages a modest fee to compensate for time away from family. Therefore, I have established a rate approximately half that of standard industry charges. I am happy to discuss flexible arrangements for those with limited financial resources. My focus is on providing guidance and empowering you to resolve issues independently, and I will not charge for educational support.

Please feel free to contact me at (778) 819-6841 if you require assistance. I look forward to the opportunity to serve you.
